BUILT AROUND THE FOUNDER’S REALITY

Less overwhelm. More purposeful progress.

You can work hard and still feel stuck: too much advice, too many features, too little evidence, and nobody helping you question your own assumptions. The weight is real. You are allowed to need structure.

“I don’t know where to start.”

Discover helps you turn a broad idea into a specific customer problem and opportunity you can explain.

“I’m afraid of building the wrong thing.”

Validate helps you test risky assumptions before adding more features or committing more resources.

“Everything feels urgent.”

Build, Launch and Scale turn ambition into scoped work, measured learning and decisions about what comes next.

Why this studio exists

To turn “What do I do now?” into a manageable action: understand it, do the work, challenge it, and carry the result into the next lesson. Your worksheets become a venture record you can review and improve.

The Academy provides a learning structure. Customers, real-world testing and qualified support still matter. A completed worksheet is progress, not proof of demand or a promise of funding.

ONE CONNECTED JOURNEY

Every phase answers a founder question.

5 phases · 20 modules · 100 lessons. Each phase contains four modules of five lessons. Module 00 prepares you; it is separate from the 100 lessons.

01 · LESSONS 01–20 · DISCOVER

“Which problem is worth pursuing?”

Understand entrepreneurship, find opportunities, shape an idea and evaluate it. Carry a focused opportunity and your assumptions into Validate.

02 · LESSONS 21–40 · VALIDATE

“What do customers and evidence tell me?”

Discover customers, understand the market, test the business model and decide whether to build. Carry findings, limits and unresolved conditions into Build.

03 · LESSONS 41–60 · BUILD

“What is the smallest useful version?”

Design and develop the product, establish the business and prepare for launch. Scope → essential features → user flow → build approach → prototype. Then test usability, define requirements and review readiness.

04 · LESSONS 61–80 · LAUNCH

“How do I reach customers and learn?”

Prepare for market, build commercial systems, launch and learn, and measure traction. Use actual behaviour and results to improve your next decisions.

05 · LESSONS 100 · SCALE

“How do I grow responsibly?”

Build for growth, develop the organization, address capital and governance, and consider long-term venture strategy. Growth follows readiness and evidence; it is not automatic.

Available here: Module 00 and Lessons 01–100. All 100 lessons are interactive. The sidebar shows every phase, module and lesson, plus local progress. Earlier adapted lessons have a documented reconciliation record.
